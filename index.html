    Welcome to AI Vision

    Landmarks, the human body, engines and machines made of a quarter-million particles of light, controlled by your hand.

    • 🫰
      Snap to summonSnap your fingers and the particles appear. Make a fist to form the model.
    • ✋
      Open to exploreOpen your hand to explode organs and engines into every named part.
    • ☝️
      Point to learnPoint at a part to see what it does. Pinch to pull it out.
    • 🙌
      Twist, tilt and zoomTurn your hand to rotate the model. Use two hands to zoom. Show ✌ for the next model.

    Hand tracking runs on this device. Video never leaves your computer.
